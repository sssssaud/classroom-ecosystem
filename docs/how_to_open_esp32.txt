ESP32 Classroom Monitor - kaise open karna hai

1. ESP32 ko power do
   - USB cable se phone charger, power bank, ya laptop me laga do.
   - 10-20 seconds wait karo.

2. LEDs dekho
   - Blue ON = ESP32 chal raha hai.
   - Green solid ON = normal state.
   - Red blink + buzzer = gas/air alert.

3. ESP32 ka hotspot connect karo
   - Phone/laptop ki WiFi settings kholo.
   - WiFi name dhundo: classroom-node
   - Is WiFi se connect ho jao.
   - Password nahi hai, agar phone warning de "No internet", tab bhi connected rehna.

4. Dashboard open karo
   - Browser kholo: Chrome/Safari/Firefox.
   - Address bar me likho:

     http://192.168.4.1

   - Agar ye na khule, ye try karo:

     http://classroom.local

5. Important
   - ESP32 hotspot internet nahi deta. Ye normal hai.
   - Phone bole "No internet" to mobile data par switch mat karna.
   - WiFi classroom-node se connected rehna zaroori hai.

6. MQ135 calibration
   - Clean air me dashboard ka Calibrate button dabao.
   - MQ135 ko best readings ke liye 24-48 hours burn-in chahiye.
